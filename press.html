About the game

Swipe up to kick. Read the wind, the weather and the distance, then split the uprights. Start at 20 yards and work your way out to the 80-yard Legend kick. Play a full Career with 8 fictional teams, take on the Daily Storm, or pass one phone around the room for game night.

Features

  • Easy to learn swipe kicking with real wind, rain and snow
  • Career mode with quarters, playoffs and a coach who learns to trust you
  • 30+ extra modes: Wind Climb, Ring Toss, Field Goal Golf, Hot Potato, 2-on-2 Team Kick and more
  • Daily Storm, Daily Puzzle Kick and weekly challenges
  • Pass & Play party modes for families
  • Comfort options: high-visibility ball, aim by ear, steady camera, reduced flashing, dyslexia-friendly font
