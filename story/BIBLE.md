# Field Goal Frenzy · Story Mode · SERIES BIBLE
### "The Long Way Through the Uprights": one 16-season movie
**This file is the single source of truth.** If story1.js, SHOTLIST.md, VOICES.md or the outline (`fgf-notes/STORY-MODE-OUTLINE-v2.md`) disagree with it, this file wins. Fix the other file, or bring the change to Kirk.
Rating: family-friendly. No real people, teams, schools or leagues. The pro league is the **Frenzy Football League (FFL)**, and its title game is the **Frenzy Cup**.

---
## 1. Fixed facts (canon)
- **Calendar:** Season 1 is the kicker's junior year at age 16. Each season is one year. Season 16 is the final FFL season at age 31.
  - High school (S1–S2) plays Friday nights. College (S3–S6) plays Saturdays.
  - FFL (S7–S16) plays Sunday afternoons, Thursday nights, Monday nights and Sunday Night. Sunday Night is the biggest stage, and the Frenzy Cup is played on a Sunday Night.
- **Hometown:** Hollow Creek, a river-country paper-mill town.
  - The mill-closing letter arrives at the end of S1. The mill closes December 1 of S1 and restarts as a recycling plant in S11.
  - The water tower gets a mural of the kicker in S10. A flood hits in S12, and the town rebuilds.
- **High schools:**
  - Hollow Creek High **Hawks** (red and gold).
  - Ridgeline High **Rattlers**, the archrival across the river (forest green and black). Not "Rams"; that's an NFL name.
  - Other S1 opponents: Mill Valley Millers, Cedar Bluff Bobcats, Prairie Creek Plowmen, Ironwood Foxes.
- **Colleges:**
  - Hometown State Huskies (40 minutes away).
  - Mountain University Bighorns.
  - Coastal Tech Mariners.
  - Prairie Valley College Pioneers.
  - Lakeshore College Loons.
  - Conference: the Sixteen Rivers Conference. Bowls: Golden Harvest Bowl and Lighthouse Bowl. Title game: the Collegiate Crown. Kicking award: the Silver Upright Award.
- **FFL teams:**
  - Harbor City Gulls (the kicker's first team).
  - Summit City Yetis.
  - River City Kingfishers (the hometown team, 30 minutes from Hollow Creek).
  - Sunburst City Scorpions.
  - Ironwood Owls, Glacier Bay Walruses, Prairie Plains Stampede, Neon Harbor Comets.
- **FFL records:** the longest field goal is **63 yards**, set by Gus Hollander in his final season, two years before S1. The consecutive-makes record is 44, held by Bram Stoll.
- **Heirloom: Old Faithful.** Pop's beat-up old lucky leather football: scuffed brown leather, worn and faded laces, a patch of tape on it. It appears in every season. (Canon change from Kirk, Oct 20 2026; it was formerly an orange kicking tee.)

## 2. Characters (look · wardrobe · voice · how they change)
The **voice** line is copied word for word from `VOICES.md`. Ages are given **as of S1**. Add 1 per season.

### The Kicker (the player)
- **Look:** custom-made by the player, so **the face is never shown** in art (back view, silhouette, or hands and feet only).
- **Wardrobe:**
  - S1–S2: plain red Hawks jersey, no number or lettering.
  - S3–S6: plain Huskies practice gear.
  - S7 on: the gear of their FFL team, kept plain with no lettering.
- **Voice:** none; the kicker never speaks aloud.
- **Arc:** age 16 to 31.
  - The **"why I kick" line** written on Pop's garage wall in S1 is the spine of the whole story.
  - The **ritual** chosen in S1 pays off in S16.

### Rae (Mom)
- **Look:** a **woman**, 38, dark hair in a messy bun, tired kind eyes.
- **Job:** night-shift **hospital custodian** at Hollow Creek Regional, plus weekend shifts at Mabel's Diner. She gave up nursing school when the kicker was born.
- **Wardrobe:** blue hospital scrubs-style work uniform, worn everywhere because she's always coming from or going to a shift.
- **Voice:** woman in her mid-30s, warm mid-low alto, slightly husky and tired from night shifts, soft rural Midwestern accent, unhurried pace. Her emotion swings from dry teasing to breaking-voice tenderness.
- **Signature lines:** "I don't need to see the kick. I just need to hear the crowd." In S1 this appears as "I didn't even see it. I just heard it."
- **Changes:**
  - S1–S4: messy bun, the scrubs uniform.
  - Goes back to nursing school at night in S5–S6; adds glasses for study scenes.
  - Graduates as a nurse in S8; her scrubs turn **teal** with a nurse badge.
  - Gray streaks from S10. Retires from night shifts in S15 and wears cardigans after that.

### Pop (Walter, Grandpa)
- **Look:** Rae's father, **72**, short white stubble, weathered hands.
- **Wardrobe:** red-and-black flannel and a worn cap, always.
- **Voice:** elderly man in his late 70s, low gravelly baritone, rough and grainy, slow Appalachian-Midwest drawl with long pauses between phrases. Gruff on top with quiet warmth underneath.
- **Background:** retired mill worker and former small-college kicker. Owns Old Faithful, the milk-jug drill and the Three Breaths.
- **Secret:** at 23 he turned down a pro tryout to stay home with his sick wife and baby Rae.
- **Changes:**
  - S1–S2: on his feet.
  - S3: a cane.
  - S4: hospital bed, the "Pop Game", then he **passes away peacefully**. He leaves a sealed letter "for the night of the biggest kick."
  - S5 on: present only through objects (cap on the garage nail, thermos at Gus's farm, his voice in memory or voicemail).
  - His secret surfaces in S14–S15, and the letter is read on Frenzy Cup night in S16.

### Jojo (younger sibling)
- **Look:** 9 in S1, shaggy dark bowl cut, gap-toothed grin. Gender is set by the player; the default art is gender-neutral.
- **Wardrobe:** oversized plain red hoodie.
- **Voice (child, S1–S3):** 9-year-old child, high bright gender-neutral voice, a little raspy from yelling, fast excited pace that tumbles over itself, giggly and dramatic.
  - From S4 (age 12) the voice lowers each season.
  - From S9 it becomes the "reporter voice": clear and quick, with the same giggle underneath.
- **Changes:**
  - 9 to 24 across the series.
  - Hairbrush "sideline reporter" mic and family **scrapbook** (S1–S2).
  - Braces and a camera at 12–14.
  - Studies journalism around S8 (age 16+).
  - A real sports reporter by S12. Covers the final game from the press box in S16.

### Puppy (the family dog)
- **Look:** a **female**, small, fluffy **Pomeranian-husky mix** with a thick **black, grey and tan** coat, pointed ears and husky face markings. She is Kirk's real dog; the name is fixed as "Puppy".
- **Origin:** she follows the band bus home after Week 1 of S1. Jojo names her and keeps her.
- **Changes:**
  - Puppy in S1–S2.
  - Full grown from S3, and she chases every missed kick.
  - Gray muzzle from S10.
  - 14 years old and slow in S15, sleeping under the practice net.
  - S16: asleep on Rae's porch at the parade.
  - She makes no sounds beyond whines, barks and panting.

### Coach D (Darnell Okafor)
- **Look:** Hollow Creek High head coach, a **Black man**, 44 in S1, short cropped hair and a trimmed beard. Calm, stern posture.
- **Wardrobe:** plain navy windbreaker with a whistle on a cord.
- **Voice:** Black man in his mid-40s, deep resonant bass-baritone, smooth and controlled, Southern-tinged Midwestern accent, measured clipped pace, calm authority that rarely rises, dry humor.
- **Background:** former military cook. Runs the barefoot field walk.
- **Changes:**
  - Gives the kicker the captain patch (S2).
  - Turns the stadium lights on in a bathrobe on prom night (S3, opening).
  - Gray beard from S10.
  - In the coaching epilogue, he hands the kicker his whistle.

### Wes Tilly (snapper, best friend)
- **Look:** 15 in S1, a **sophomore**, one grade below the kicker. Quiet, lanky, shaggy light-brown hair, slouched shoulders.
- **Wardrobe:** plain red practice jersey with no number.
- **Voice:** 15-year-old boy, soft light tenor with occasional crack, mumbly, slow and shy pace, hesitant, almost whispering. Steadier from S4 on.
- **Changes:**
  - Learns to long snap off the garage door in S1.
  - Stays at Hollow Creek for his senior year in S3, then **walks on at Hometown State one year after the kicker (S4)**.
  - College snapper S4–S6.
  - Best man or best friend at the wedding in S9, where he gives the toast.
  - Slouch gone and a short beard by the pro years.

### Gus Hollander ("Iron Toe")
- **Look:** FFL kicking legend, **36 in S1**. Broad, short beard going early gray at the chin, always a toothpick.
- **Career:** 14 seasons with the Harbor City Gulls. Holds the 63-yard record and is **still active** in S1, running off-season clinics.
- **Wardrobe:** plain green ball cap with no logo, faded gray windbreaker.
- **Voice:** man in his late 30s, big booming warm baritone, slightly hoarse, plain Great Lakes accent, confident relaxed pace, kindly but no-nonsense. Grows raspier with age.
- **Changes:**
  - Pop secretly writes him letters starting in S1.
  - Competes against the kicker for the Gulls job in S7 and is cut.
  - Goat farmer after that; his goat Scout appears from S11.
  - Arrives with a goat in the truck at age 48 (S13).
  - Leaves a final voicemail before the last Frenzy Cup (S16).

## 3. Timeline: 16 seasons
Arc, branch points, choices that carry forward (⚑), and threads each season must pay off later (→).

**S1 · "Milk Jugs and Night Shifts" (HS junior, 16).**
- **Arc:** Soccer has been cut. Pop gives the kicker Old Faithful, and the kicker writes the "why I kick" line on his garage wall. Coach D's barefoot tryout follows. Wes is invited in, and Puppy follows the band bus home. The Fog Game happens, and Pop nearly tells his secret, then teaches the Three Breaths. A 40-yard call comes at Ironwood. Rivalry Night against Ridgeline ends 11 years of losses or makes it 12. Rae is at the fence. The cliffhanger: the mill is closing, and Rae on the phone says the kid may have to quit football to work.
- **Branches:** rivalry win or loss (with overtime).
- **⚑ Carries forward:** the why-line, the ritual, Wes invited, Gus's tip or signature, the 40-yard call, Dez response, polaroid caption, Milk Jug Challenge, and the meters (fan, coach, hidden family).
- **→ Must pay off:** the mill closing (S2), Pop's fog secret (S14–15), Gus "knew a kicker from Hollow Creek" (Pop's letters, S7/S14), and the why-line (S16).

**S2 · "Captain of the Hawks" (HS senior, 17).**
- **Arc:** The mill fallout: the kicker sweeps Mabel's Diner at 5 a.m. to keep the house. Captain vote, Homecoming, the first nickname from the S1 choices, recruiting, and Ridgeline again.
- **Cliffhanger:** on Signing Day, Coastal Tech gives its kicker spot to Dez.
- **⚑ Carries forward:** the work-vs-football choice (pays off S1), and the nickname.
- **→ Must pay off:** Dez's Coastal Tech spot (S5), and the hat chosen (S3).

**S3 · "Walk-On" (College Y1, 18).**
- **Arc:** Prom night, with Coach D's lights. A walk-on tryout under Pike; Brody is the starter; laundry-room practice with Marcus; the Thanksgiving holiday chapter.
- **Cliffhanger:** Brody is hurt before the Lighthouse Bowl.
- **⚑ Carries forward:** the college choice (Hometown State is canon for the shared beats), and the Brody relationship.

**S4 · "Old Faithful" (College Y2, 19).**
- **Arc:** The Lighthouse Bowl kick and the first "Next" note. A scholarship; Rae signs with shaking hands. Wes arrives as snapper, and Sam is met. The Pop Game, then **Pop passes**, leaving the sealed letter.
- **Cliffhanger:** Coastal Tech offers a transfer; Dez texts "don't come here."
- **→ Must pay off:** the sealed letter (S16), and Pop's cap on the garage nail (a recurring object).

**S5 · "The Kicker Who Hated Me" (Y3, 20).**
- **Arc:** Dez is injured and dropped. The hospital waiting-room talk with Dez. June at the clinic. Rae starts nursing school.
- **Cliffhanger:** Dez's "Watch me" video, and the kicker tops the Silver Upright watch list.

**S6 · "The Silver Upright and the Crown" (Y4, 21).**
- **Arc:** Captain. Agent choice ⚑ (Mo or Brock). Dez holds for the kicker after practice. Collegiate Crown against Coastal Tech.
- **Cliffhanger:** Pike's verdict to the FFL scout.

**S7 · "Draft Night" (FFL Y1, 22).**
- **Arc:** The Combine score sets the draft range ⚑ (drafted or undrafted). Draft night in Hollow Creek's living room with Puppy at the kicker's feet. Gulls camp duel with Gus. The Operation: Pip and Hollis.
- **Cliffhanger:** an iced 49-yard wild-card kick, with voicemails chosen by the Heart meter, and Sam's "I have to tell you something."

**S8 · "Sophomore Slump" (Y2, 23).**
- **Arc:** Wild-card result. Sam's teaching job. The yips. Rae becomes a nurse. Dez is an undrafted grinder.
- **Cliffhanger:** Hollis is hurt: "Don't wait for me."

**S9 · "Prove It" (Y3, 24).**
- **Arc:** Kip is the new snapper, Rhythm drops to zero. Contract choice ⚑ (extension or prove-it). The wedding in Hollow Creek square, Gus officiating, Wes's toast. Tank's injury.
- **Cliffhanger:** trade rumor; a call from Pike.

**S10 · "The Trade" (Y4, 25).**
- **Arc:** Deadline trade to Pike's Yetis. Riley is born. The water-tower mural.
- **Cliffhanger:** hip "pop" in an overtime playoff game.

**S11 · "Rush Back or Rest" (Y5, 26).**
- **Arc:** Rush back or rest ⚑ (shapes leg range through S16). Volunteer shift at Rae's hospital. The mill reopens as a recycling plant (pays off the S1 mill thread). Pip's humming stops.
- **Cliffhanger:** free agency, and one offer is from the team the kicker swore off.

**S12 · "Bidding War" (Y6, 27).**
- **Arc:** Yetis, Gulls or Rennard's Scorpions ⚑. Bea is born. The Hollow Creek flood and rebuild.
- **Cliffhanger:** June is drafted.

**S13 · "The Kid With the Cannon" (Y7, 28).**
- **Arc:** Veteran against June. Mirror practice. The kicker is cut.
- **Cliffhanger:** Gus (48) arrives with a goat: "Bring the old ball."

**S14 · "The Phoenix Season" (Y8, 29, narrated by Earl).**
- **Arc:** Rebuild on the goat farm. Pop's thermos and letters are found at Gus's. Dez gets the Kingfishers to sign the kicker. Phoenix Award.
- **→ Pays off:** Pop's secret, begun.

**S15 · "Chasing Iron Toe" (Y9, 30).**
- **Arc:** Kingfishers captain. Chasing the 63-yard and 44-straight records. Rae retires. Puppy is 14. Riley's career day; Bea's snap camp with Hollis.
- **Cliffhanger:** Hollis texts "Snap?"

**S16 · "One More Snap" (Y10, 31).**
- **Arc:** The Operation reunites. Farewell season. Frenzy Cup on Sunday Night against Rennard's Scorpions, with June kicking for them. Pop's letter is read, and the S1 ritual pays off.
- **The last scene:** Rae's Seat, the why-line answered, and Riley picks up Old Faithful.

## 4. Continuity rules
- **Style line:** use it word for word in every image prompt:
  `STYLE: painted cinematic illustration, visible soft brushwork, Americana small-town realism, muted film palette (dusty navy, faded rust, warm amber, mill-town gray-green), soft film grain, gentle volumetric light, 9:16 vertical portrait, story-game key art, no text anywhere.`
- **Shot rules:**
  - One or two figures; faces clear (except the kicker).
  - No crowds with visible faces.
  - No text, numbers or logos.
  - Gentle camera and environment motion only. The speaker's lips move; nothing else does.
- **Recurring places.** Reuse the same description every time:
  - **Hollow Creek field:** small aluminum bleachers, yellow posts, water tower and mill chimneys beyond, Friday lights.
  - **The fence:** a chain-link fence along the home sideline. Rae's spot, used in S1 and S16.
  - **Pop's garage:** single bulb, pencil height marks, Old Faithful on a nail. After S4, Pop's cap hangs on that nail.
  - **The kitchen:** salt shaker, thin wall, back door where Puppy waits.
  - **The porch:** porch light, rocking chair, coffee mug.
  - **Hospital break room:** mop bucket and a cracked phone.
- **Recurring visual beats:**
  - The milk jug.
  - Old Faithful hitting the grass before every big kick.
  - The chosen ritual.
  - Puppy chasing misses.
  - Rae's Seat glowing.
  - Jojo's hairbrush mic, which later becomes a real mic.
  - Pike's "Next" sticky notes (S4+).
  - Lou's stopwatch (S13+).
- **Puppy rule:** Puppy never speaks or lip-syncs: her mouth stays closed except for panting or a yawn; only humans speak. In any shot with Puppy and an offscreen voice, the prompt must say "the dog's mouth stays closed, the voice comes from offscreen".
- **Voice rules:**
  - Every spoken line uses that character's VOICES.md description inline.
  - The kicker never speaks.
  - Texts can be read as the sender's voice-over.
  - Characters who age (Jojo, Wes, Pop, Gus) get an updated voice line in VOICES.md at the season where it changes.
- **Names never used:** NFL, Super Bowl, real schools, real teams, "Rams", "Ravens", "Raiders", and any celebrity.
