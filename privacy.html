Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Field Goal Frenzy is a free game. We collect as little as possible.

What is stored on your device

Your progress, settings, and coins are saved in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device.

What is sent to our server

  • A random device ID, your chosen nickname, and your scores when you use online features (leaderboards, head-to-head, weekly challenge).
  • A referral or friend code if you enter one.
  • An email address only if you choose to enter it for cloud save or early-access updates.

What we do not do

No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data, no location, no contacts. There are no in-app purchases with real money.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Online features use only a nickname and a random ID.

Deleting your data

Clearing the site data in your browser removes local data. To remove server data, contact us and include your friend code.
